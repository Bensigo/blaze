# X feedback connection and collection

**Blocked by default:** No real paid X verification is approved. X documents a billing-cycle spending limit, but its configuration, scope and residual exposure have not been verified for this account. Without a current owner-reviewed receipt and explicit approval, connect, callback completion, refresh and search are blocked. No environment flag enables them. Estimates and request limits are not a spending cap. The owner can record their console review and explicitly accept unknown residual exposure for one identity attempt and one search; this is not provider-API verification or a guaranteed hard total cap.

## Provider controls and remaining exposure

As checked 29 October 2026, [X pricing](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing) says the Developer Console supports a maximum spend per billing cycle and blocks requests when reached. It also says the balance can go slightly negative; it does not quantify that overshoot here. Auto-recharge can trigger when a balance crosses below its threshold, including on the first spend when initially equal to it, and may create a new charge. Its five-minute limit and pause at zero or negative balance do not establish a zero-overshoot total budget. The per-cycle cap may be shared with other account use; its exact scope, remaining allowance, timing, fees and handling of in-flight requests need account-specific verification. A cycle limit alone does not establish an exact maximum cash exposure for this test.

The documented read-only [credit balance endpoint](https://docs.x.com/x-api/usage/get-usage-credits) returns the payer's prepaid and free balances; `total_balance` is floored at zero even when prepaid balance is negative. The [usage endpoints](https://docs.x.com/x-api/usage/introduction) also expose post consumption. These are observations, not proof of the configured spending limit, recharge settings, available cycle allowance or full cost exposure. The reviewed documentation does not identify a read-only endpoint for those settings; this is not proof that none exists. No account or API call was made to verify them.

Before a future approval, an authorized operator needs current evidence identifying the payer and app, cycle dates and remaining allowance, configured limit and scope, recharge and payment settings, balance including negative prepaid amounts, applicable fees, concurrent consumers, and a defensible bound for in-flight and overshoot charges. Bind that evidence and any approval to the exact payer, operation, amount and expiry; recheck immediately before dispatch and stop on stale, missing or conflicting evidence. The owner-reviewed receipt is tied to the workspace, configured X app credentials, named account and game, expires within one hour and permits one identity attempt and one search. The identity attempt is reserved before token exchange and atomically consumed after the exchange immediately before the identity lookup; search is atomically consumed immediately before dispatch. Database time checks reject expiry at consumption. Failed attempts remain consumed. Replacing or revoking a receipt prevents future dispatch but cannot recall an in-flight request. The status explicitly says owner reviewed, not API verified. Setting up credentials, buying credits, changing account settings or executing a test requires separate authorization.

The X feedback panel records this review with `POST /api/x-feedback-controls`. The same-origin authenticated request names the payer, evidence reference, billing-cycle limit and remaining USD, prepaid balance USD, cycle end, game and expected X account ID, and explicitly confirms auto-recharge off, shared-account consideration, acceptance of unknown fees/overshoot, and one identity and one search. The server requires the reviewed prepaid balance and remaining cycle allowance each to cover the $0.06 reference for one user and ten posts. This does not bound additional charges or overshoot. The status labels the balance owner-reviewed, not API-verified. The panel sets a 30-minute expiry; the server accepts at most one hour within the current cycle. The receipt binds the current feedback app credential hash. Its access token is not independently fingerprinted; search checks that the stored credential and connection name the approved account. Account mismatch can be discovered only after a potentially billable identity request; the attempt remains spent. A revoke request blocks later dispatches, not requests already in flight. Replacement is blocked during an attempt. A failed or ambiguous dispatched request requires the owner to confirm in X that it settled and review charges before resolving the attempt and making a new approval. If a process dies while an attempt is marked in flight, the receipt remains blocked and requires operator investigation; do not infer that it settled.

This capability is separate from X publishing. It does not post, follow, message or subscribe, and daily research does not search X. No real account, entitlement, pricing or search has been tested by this implementation.

## Consent and identity

Configure a **separate** confidential X OAuth 2.0 web app and set `X_FEEDBACK_CLIENT_ID` and `X_FEEDBACK_CLIENT_SECRET`; register the exact canonical callback `/api/social/x_feedback/callback`. This document does not authorize configuring an app or credentials. The connection screen must disclose the exact requested scopes before a user explicitly connects:

- `tweet.read`: read posts the authorized account can view;
- `users.read`: read accounts the authorized account can view;
- `offline.access`: remain connected until revoked, allowing token refresh.

These scopes can cover protected content the account can view, though this feature only invokes recent search. They do not grant publishing, media, follows or messages. X's consent screen is authoritative. The app requests PKCE and verifies the returned scopes and identity; unexpected scopes are rejected. OAuth exchange calls `GET /2/users/me`, which can incur a read charge. A connected identity proves neither search entitlement nor available credits. No search is performed to test it.

After recording an owner-reviewed receipt and approval, the client may submit `POST /api/social/x_feedback/connect` with `{ "readOnlyConsent": true }`, then navigates to the returned OAuth URL. Existing browser-bound, one-time state and workspace ownership protect the callback. Reconnection includes its owned `connectionId` and the same explicit consent. The connection's separate credential is encrypted and cannot be used as the publishing destination. Disconnect uses the existing social disconnect flow. When an access token expires, the user must explicitly reconnect before collection; collection does not silently refresh or make an extra identity request.

## Manual collection

`GET /api/games/{id}/feedback` is workspace-scoped and returns the title-derived query, a maximum of 10 results, identity state, `not_tested` search entitlement and grouped persisted findings. The `POST` requires same-origin authentication, ownership, and `{ "confirmSearchAndCost": true }`. The UI shows the actual query and explains that this authorizes **one** X recent-search request, up to 10 returned posts and a potential charge. It must require a fresh confirmation every run. There is no pagination, retry, automatic background search, model call, identity refresh or publication in this operation. A planned 6-per-hour workspace limit is a rate limit, not a spending cap; failures may still be billable.

As checked 29 October 2026, [X pricing](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing) lists $0.005 per Post read and $0.010 per User read. Ten returned posts therefore suggest $0.05 for those post reads at the listed rate, excluding identity and other unknown charges. The flow would make one search request with up to ten posts; connecting would also perform one user identity lookup, and there may be other charges. These request/resource bounds do not enforce a total spend limit. Rates, billing and eligibility can change; check the Developer Console before connecting or running. This estimate is not a guaranteed cap or a grant to buy credits, save a card, enable recharge or spend. X describes credit billing, per-resource reads, and soft same-day deduplication; entitlement, account billing configuration, and actual charges remain unknown.

The [recent search endpoint](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/search-recent-posts) is incomplete by design and may require paid access or credits. The [OAuth scopes documentation](https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/authentication/oauth-2-0/authorization-code) describes their breadth and refresh behavior. A successful call proves access only for that request. A failed call reports a sanitized HTTP status and may have incurred charges; inspect access, quota and billing before retrying.

Returned posts become same-game Finding records with source URL/date. Classification is a simple text heuristic; it may be wrong. A post containing the game URL is marked direct, otherwise relevance is possible and must be reviewed. Bug reports and requests are claims, not verified reproductions; even a direct URL requires context review. Repeated source IDs do not overwrite existing findings or owner statuses. Results do not represent all players or all discussion.
